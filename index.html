A bit about me

I’m an experience strategist and UX designer focused on solving complex, end-to-end experience challenges through systems thinking, collaboration, and human-centered design.

Over the past decade, I’ve worked across healthcare, medical devices, financial services, and higher education — helping teams bring clarity to messy systems, align around user needs, and design experiences that work in the real world.

What I’m best at:

  • Organizing complexity into clear, actionable experiences
  • Connecting business goals, user needs, and operational realities
  • Facilitating alignment across cross-functional teams
  • Designing thoughtful, scalable experience ecosystems
  • Turning ambiguity into momentum

I’m especially energized by work that requires deep partnership, long-term thinking, and meaningful impact over time.

Outside of work, I’m usually spending time with my family, exploring great coffee shops and restaurants, baking something new, or working on creative side projects.
